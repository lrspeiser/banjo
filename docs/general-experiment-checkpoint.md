# General experiment authoring checkpoint

The [execution plan](general-experiment-plan.md) preserves the full platform
scope. Implemented here: composable initial-state authoring, typed model setup
selection, bounded controls, diagnostic recordings, archived result restoration,
and live randomized request evaluation. No new constitutive solver is claimed.

## What changed

- `drop_test` controls target dimensions, projectile size/shape, height sweeps,
  impact offset, resolution and support. Glass, oak and iron remain matched.
  Clamped edges use the existing volume network; free ground-supported targets
  currently use rigid bodies. Projectiles begin at exactly the requested clearance.
- `scene_test` composes catalog objects with explicit position, orientation,
  velocity, spin, gravity, ground friction and optional network pinning/resolution.
  Network scenes retain strict damage-limit rejection. No arbitrary new law or
  material is generated by GPT.
- The model now selects one typed setup; a deterministic lowering step fills the
  executable language's legacy fields. Early test batches demonstrated why prompt
  instructions alone could not prevent duplicate/conflicting fields.
- Requested fidelity is explicit: calibrated simulations are unavailable and
  blocked independently of the selected setup. Experimental inputs remain intact.
  Invalid generated UI falls back to standard controls with a warning; physics
  validation still rejects invalid inputs. No automatic paid repair call occurs.
- `GET /api/schema` exposes the executable declaration and budgets. Completed,
  blocked and failed jobs persist; known job links recover after server restart.
  Restored native-window launch is disabled; embedded recordings remain available.

## Thin glass: admission is not physical validation

The legacy playground imposed a 12 mm minimum, while native geometry accepts
4 mm. The new drop builder admits an explicit 6 mm volumetric diagnostic with
appropriate warnings. It does not add a thin-shell model or tempered-glass
manufacturing stress.

6 mm targets, an 80 mm iron ball, 50 mm drop clearance, clamped edges and 4 × 4 × 2

fractures and full rollback of the failing tick. All three materials share the

cases must return blocked. A solver-limit recording counts as request execution
coverage but not completed or validated physics. Preserve failed earlier batches

## Next work

Authoring stages are operational within declared limits. The thin-structure
representation, unified dynamic contact/deformation/fracture solver and realtime
qualification remain unfinished. Next require bending/contact oracles, conserved
work/reactions, spatial and temporal refinement, fragment handoff, and calibrated

outside this initial-state authoring slice.
