# Gathered constituent input and paid tool forming — October 5, 2026

This host checkpoint adds a private native-cell input route to the existing
finite shaping process. It advances collection-to-tool reuse under an explicitly
declared reduction. It does not qualify stone fracture, calibrated machining,
state-preserving deformation, grain, wear or whole-world conservation. The
[ground audit](ground-matter-audit.md) and
[solid reference](solid-matter-reference-checkpoint.md) retain their boundaries.

## Material and formation contract

`mcp/matter_fabrication.py::receive_matter` accepts only the trusted native
`banjo.ground-matter-transfer.v1` receipt. The host must save native collection,
the matching ground withdrawal and the receiving candidate together. It retains
the complete source-cell packet, source IDs, clipped dimensions, source centers,
material, density, mass and declared source work in `matter_lots`. A matching
bulk receipt preserves the existing exported-volume audit and recovery tools.
This is one material account represented by two linked records, not two credits.
No extra concrete rack credit occurs.

The selectable **Stone field pick** uses the ordinary generic field-pick
compiler, point/grip frames, native constituent measurement and fixed mounts.
Its 0.8 m oak handle and 0.32 m concrete-surrogate head have 0.08 m square
sections. The name stone does not introduce a natural-stone catalog law;
terrain rock continues to use the existing 2,400 kg/m³ concrete surrogate.
The oak handle comes from finite catalog wood stock and remains a declared
cold-reservoir input. Its historical wood cells are not reconstructed.

`plan_make` reads unspent native rock belonging to the requester and allocates
it against the exact native quote. Planning does not mutate material or energy.
`start_make` rechecks those source allocations and reserves them in the same
saved candidate as the paid job. Source state remains in the job and final
installed receipt. A different player, exhausted lot, changed allocation or
changed source state refuses before any debit. Repeated requests retain the
existing job receipt. Legacy aggregate rock and ore cannot become concrete
inputs merely through a renamed material.

Forming uses the existing `work_j_kg` coefficient, station power, efficiency,
finite supply and heat/cooling ledger. This is **declared cold-stock shaping**,
not contact-measured cutting. It can resample exact 5 cm source volumes into the
accepted 4 cm product grid; each formed cell records fractional original source
IDs, volume and mass. Source-to-output mappings plus separately retained
offcut fractions cover every consumed source fraction. Original damage/history
is retained as provenance; formed cold geometry does not claim to preserve its
strain or recoverable energy. Joining retains the existing fixed-interface
strength and bending-law limits. The source work from excavation is historical
evidence, not another credit to the forming supply.

Current raw holdings appear in recipe material readiness and the reviewed paid
quote. Raw material cannot be double counted as a SQL catalog rack balance.
Stored native constituent input stays private after restart; its spent portion
is excluded from later raw retrieval and manufacture. Aggregate returns or
machine input deliveries lose per-cell selection, so the remainder of such a
lot is explicitly excluded from further constituent forming.
