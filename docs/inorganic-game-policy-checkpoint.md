# Playable inorganic material policy — October 5, 2026

Implementation based on main `c67f1339919eadfd2a51b18339512610b5f9de48`; publication and demo revision are recorded below. This follows the owner's decision to defer organic-material physics. It is an admission boundary, not material realism certification.

## Player contract

- New-game terrain/resources, starter equipment, Build sources, goals, material selectors and Market use metals and inorganic components. The compact first pick has an aluminum haft and iron arm; no wood, rubber, plastic or leather component is admitted.
- Native physical body presets admitted by the host are iron, aluminum, glass, alumina ceramic, ice and concrete. Individual engine lanes still impose their own stricter admission checks. Raw resource policy also permits soil/sand/clay/rock/water, ores, copper/wire, limestone, cement and coal. Coal is a resource; no native coal solid or combustion law is claimed.
- Shared Lab/world/character guidance receives explicit material instructions. Authoritative source checks guard chat edits, library writes, fabrication quotes and installation; instructions alone cannot bypass admission. Rejected edits roll back the draft. Declared density and geometry remain physical inputs, never substituted based on a display name.
- Existing organic world saves are preserved and refused before an active engine or clock starts. The response offers New game; that request can leave a retired world without reopening it. There is no automatic wood-to-metal exchange, reset of private stock or rewrite of native snapshots.
- Old saved designs/library stock remain in their files/database but are hidden from game selection. Research/laboratory scenes retain genuine oak/rubber fixtures and glass/oak/iron comparisons. They are outside the playable catalog.
