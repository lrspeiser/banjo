# CUDA fault collection and Newton failure evidence — October 9, 2026

## Implemented

The `/coupled` 3D laboratory now explains refused intervals: number of rejected trials, whether the scene was restored, individual subdivision reasons, Newton residual/tolerance and line-search attempts. The downloadable experiment record contains the actual last rejected poses, velocities, forces, work ledger and material history, plus the initial body/interface arrays and last assembled Jacobian/singular values. A rejected candidate never becomes rendered physical motion. The displayed scene remains the accepted snapshot.

`TrialFailure` carries bounded diagnostic data. At most 128 solve attempts are allowed per interval, 24 Newton iterations per solve and eight search probes per iteration; the existing capacities and 30-second interval guard remain. The Jacobian is the last assembled Newton-base matrix; on iteration-budget exhaustion it can precede the final search candidate. It is not claimed to be that candidate's tangent. Diagnostic transfers occur on failure and are included in the existing interval wall budget. A poisoned CUDA context still cannot confirm device rollback.

CUDA fault collection now uses one block per candidate and an **integer** minimum to preserve the earliest contribution failure and the original precedence: contribution fault, body finiteness, material history, poses, ledger. This replaces serial scans by one thread per candidate. No force/work arithmetic, ordered floating sums, constitutive inputs, solver initial guess, tolerances or physical admission gates changed. CPU material-world executable is unchanged.

## What the stronger impact failures actually show

[Four-material diagnostic comparison](evidence/gpu-newton-diagnostics/comparative-refusals.json) was captured with the first diagnostic port, before the integer fault optimization; its distinct source SHA and scope are retained. Conditions: iron ball 0.1 kg, surface clearance 5 mm, host dt 1/960 s, target duration 0.104167 s. Oak and iron finish without fracture/yield. Glass stops at 0.034375 s after 66 failed Newton line searches. Ice stops at 0.0958333 s with 63 line-search and two iteration-budget refusals. Failed intervals restore their prior accepted scenes.

[Final-source glass replay](evidence/gpu-newton-diagnostics/glass-refusal.json) confirms the same 0.034375 s accepted time, 129th trial-limit refusal, 66 failed line searches and 62 private accepted microsteps rolled back. It takes 10.88 wall seconds. The last failed solve uses dt 3.2552e-5 s and stalls near equation norm 0.534, far above the unchanged 1e-10 tolerance. Its rejected candidate enters large openings/complete damage at many interfaces while the retained accepted scene remains intact. This is a failed solve, not simulated fracture and not an accepted material outcome.

[Tangent probe](evidence/gpu-newton-diagnostics/tangent-probe.json) at a rejected glass Newton base compares weighted finite-difference scales 1e-9 through 1e-14. Changing that scale does not produce a reducing step within the existing search probes. Agreement with a directional finite difference is not an analytical derivative proof. Private regularized trust-step and reversed-velocity initial-guess experiments also fail to qualify the impact; neither is installed. Work limits, material strength and conservation tolerances were not increased to force a passing result.
