## Comparative-material rule

Always run material-dependent experiments with **glass and oak**, retaining **iron** as the third reference case and expanding the set as substances are added. Keep geometry, initial motion, gravity/support, resolution, timestep and solver settings identical for comparisons unless a declared experiment intentionally holds mass or inertia fixed. Report every material separately. Keep material-neutral analytical oracles alongside the comparisons. A preset name, color or a successful build is not material realism.

The experimental material-network v2 path is property-driven by declared material and shape data. It supports occupied-cell boxes/ellipsoids and rigid convex wedge tools under ABI `banjo-network-2`, with bounds of 1024 cells, 20,000 links and 32 objects. Its directional axial lattice, local cohesive damage, and axial perfect-plastic demonstrator are implementation evidence only; they do not establish full wood grain, calibrated iron plasticity, tomato realism, or global realtime behavior. See [network runtime checkpoint](network-runtime-checkpoint.md).
