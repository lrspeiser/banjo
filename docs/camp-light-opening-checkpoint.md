## Implemented

- Shared guidance links to the remaining output of an actual owned watched
  batch. The destination uses the current output pile and available amount.
  Observation reserves no goods: another player can collect shared output.
  Normal distance, ownership and paired durable-save checks still apply.
- Recipes includes a compact Camp light with three exact rigid components,
  a 5 W lamp and an explicitly connected battery. Its starting charge is
  paid by manufacture. This is an optional useful product, not a new mandatory
  goal, prerequisite, damage model or chemistry simulation.
- After the workshop chapter, sufficient personal processed glass recommends
  this design. Shared starter glass alone does not trigger it. Explicit
  selected designs and accepted owned work retain their existing priority.
- Actual paid admission plus private Inventory/native lamp state guide equip,
  placement and switching on. An empty battery reports a recharge requirement.
  A display name awards neither mastery nor a free product. A lit owned light
  does not recommend buying or making another one.
