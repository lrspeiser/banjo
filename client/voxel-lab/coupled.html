Model and work account

Nine connected 10 mm cubes on two fixed supports. Gravity, material traction and compliant contact solve together. Glass, oak and ice use declared mixed mode cohesive interfaces; iron uses a six mode plastic connector. Cell mass and inertia come from geometry and density.

Contact uses frictionless linear compliance. Box surfaces use face quadrature; edge collisions and continuous collision detection remain unqualified. Oak has no grain model; iron is not continuum J2 plasticity. No thermal or fluid laws here.

Every physical substep must pass momentum, angular momentum and energy gates. Failed intervals roll back and stop. The log includes actual intermediate poses, velocities, material history, external support reactions and numerical return excess. The latter is not heat.

The parallel CUDA pipeline evaluates independent material/contact contributions and gathers them in reference order. Its tested low-energy trajectories match the serial reference exactly. This prototype remains much slower than realtime. Camera rendering is independent of calculations. No canned fracture motion or extrapolated physics.
