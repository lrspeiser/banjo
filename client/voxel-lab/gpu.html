Physics boundary / record

Newton 1.6.1 · Warp 1.18.0 · FP32 XPBD. Density determines mass and inertia. Restitution and friction are declared global contact parameters, not calibrated material laws. No fracture, plasticity, grain, thermal behavior or continuous collision detection.

Every calculated substep is audited for finite values and energy creation. Refused batches retain the previous accepted scene. Ground reactions and numerical/contact losses are not yet fully accounted. Passing the admission gate is not proof of physical realism. The block stack currently fails.

Rendering interpolates two accepted poses; it never predicts impact outcomes. Speed includes cold graph captures. The end-to-end value also includes IPC, publication and logging.
