Physics boundary / record

Newton 1.6.1 XPBD or ovphysx 0.6.3 TGS · Warp 1.18.0 · FP32 CUDA. Density determines mass and inertia. Restitution and friction are numeric contact parameters, not calibrated material laws. No bonded fracture, plasticity, grain, thermal behavior or continuous collision detection.

Every calculated substep is audited for finite values and energy creation. Refused batches retain the previous accepted scene and stop. PhysX normal-force impulse accounts are logged; friction is missing from that ledger. Ground reactions and numerical/contact losses are not fully qualified. Newton's stack fails; PhysX bounce and complete friction accounts remain unqualified; the zero-bounce/friction PhysX stack is a separate control.

Rendering interpolates accepted poses; it never predicts outcomes. Speed includes actual readback and audits; end-to-end also includes IPC, publication and logging.
