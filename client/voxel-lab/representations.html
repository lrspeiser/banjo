One ball, two representations

The same occupied matter travels as one rigid owner and expands back into its original elements. This checks geometry and transfer; internal forces and impact are not connected here yet.

No contact or fracture solver in this workbench. GPU contact tests remain available in the impact lab.
Geometry and transfer limits

The requested sphere is approximated by an adaptive cube union. Larger interior cubes reduce element count; boundary cubes provide finer geometry. Empty space remains empty. Mass follows occupied volume and installed material density, with no mass correction to hide geometry error.

The rigid proxy retains this exact union, full inertia, element identities and interface state. Only negligible elastic and relative kinetic energy permit collapse. Disconnected matter needs separate owners. Gravity and isotropic free spin are calculated on the CPU; these are accepted physical poses, not a contact animation.

Flight stops at 20 cm conservative clearance above a ground plane. This reference does not simulate a collision, cracks, dents, thermal effects or flowing matter. It is not a measurement of realtime impact performance.
