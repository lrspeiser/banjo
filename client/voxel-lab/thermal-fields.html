What this does—and what remains

Native CPU calculations evolve thermal energy and finite fuel, oxygen and product masses. Ice has a declared latent-heat plateau at 273.15 K. Oak contains explicit demonstration fuel; names do not determine reactions.

The geometry stays fixed. Melting does not yet create flowing water; heating does not yet expand or weaken the mechanics. There is no flame animation, smoke, transport, calibrated combustion, radiation or environmental cooling. Fuel and oxygen stay in closed local reservoirs; retained products replace consumed reactants.

Pairwise exact conduction is composed with symmetric graph splitting; phase exchanges use conservative backward Euler. This is a bounded experimental field adapter, not a complete continuum thermal world.
