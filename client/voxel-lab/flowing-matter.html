Law and limits

Flat-bed Saint-Venant hydrostatic equations; conservative Rusanov face fluxes and reflecting walls. CFL ≤ 0.2. Mechanical energy lost numerically is reported as numerical loss, never called heat. The columns can merge and split their volume through face fluxes; they are not solid cubes.

No vertical jet, turbulence, viscosity, capillarity, wheel force coupling, melting or freezing. This reference does not make molten glass, oak or iron by changing a label. The material input is an explicitly declared liquid density.
