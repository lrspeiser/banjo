          What happened

            The machine as data

            The base declaration, before your tray pieces are added. Edit it and build; the server checks it and the engine calculates the rest. Tray pieces are saved separately with the design.

              How honest is each station?

              Calculated uses the engine's tested laws for rigid bodies, hinges and DC circuits. Experimental is calculated but not calibrated against real material tests: oak burning and its loss of strength, and how glass breaks (whether it breaks is computed; the number of pieces is not calibrated). Not built is not in this shared world yet.

              Breaking can take the engine longer than real time; the clock then shows how far world time is behind.

              How to play

              1. Choose a challenge. Read its goal beside the scene. Green boxes mark destinations; other challenges measure things like light, power or water.
              2. Add a piece. Click the pictured card in Your tray, beside the scene controls. If there are several pieces, use its arrows to browse them. Browsing does not place a piece. In the Sandbox, Add a piece also opens the full piece library.
              3. Move and tweak it. Drag the blue ghost or use the arrows. A click or tap leaves it where it is and returns to View; dragging the ghost resumes Move. Drag empty space to turn the view, or use a right/middle mouse drag or Ctrl+drag even over the piece. On a phone, drag near the piece to move it and use two fingers to turn and zoom the view. Change its size and angle with the piece controls. A red ghost tells you why it cannot fit. Set down confirms your placement.
              4. Try your machine. Press Run it to place the piece you are adjusting and start the machine. A piece that does not fit stays red so you can move it. Pause stops the run; Set up again restores your setup. Tap a piece to move or change it. Solar collectors connect by clicking Output, then the receiving Input.
              5. Keep building. Beat a challenge's energy par for three stars. In the Sandbox, choose a prebuilt design or Add New Design, name your build and use Save version to keep a setup you can reopen.
              6. Draw wires. Where a wire is available, click or tap one labelled terminal, then another to connect them. Use + and − on batteries and bulbs, and In and Out on switches. Esc cancels a wire in progress. Prepared wires and your wires follow the same floor routes; crossings do not connect. Yellow wires show measured current, including in the flow preview.
              7. Need a hand? Use Ask for help beside the game. Place it for me attempts a solution, worth one star at most.

              Keyboard: arrows move a piece; Page Up/Down change its height; Q/E turn, R/F tip and Z/C roll. Enter sets it down. Esc switches between moving and looking around. Ctrl+Z undoes a change.
