Before and after share a camera. Geometry is actual native geometry; fracture colors identify connected pieces without moving them apart. The ball/sheet experiment uses 72 sheet cells and 32 ball cells. Rigid mode has no internal damage.

Water / wheel releases a finite 5.12 litre column: 80 equal-volume parcels with gravity, experimental SPH pressure and viscosity, and native parcel/paddle contact. The unpowered wheel has a real hinge, eight paddles and eight spokes. Its mass/inertia follow the chosen material. Paddles stay rigid; wood grain, bending and fracture are unsupported. This is a laboratory oak comparison, not a wood gameplay item.

Water uses reduced sound speed (20 m/s), no tensile pressure, surface tension or boundary density correction. Splash shapes and fluid compressibility are uncalibrated. The pour is finite, not a continual source. Support, contact and numerical losses remain unclosed.

Deformable stiffness needs tiny substeps and is much slower than real time. Contact + 14 m/s starts with that declared velocity; it does not simulate the preceding fall. Short fracture observations do not establish settled shards. Metals use axial plasticity; realistic unloaded plate dents need bending and unloading validation.
