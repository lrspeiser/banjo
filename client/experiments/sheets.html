What is simulated?

Live native contact against a deformable axial-bond sheet. Broken bonds and cell positions come from the CPU solver. The pick is a rigid assembly with material-derived mass and contact properties. The stands show ideal fixed supports; post collisions are not implemented.

Plastic extension is an axial reference response; a calibrated metal dent after unloading is not yet validated. Oak grain, rubber hyperelasticity, tool breakage, fragment collisions, gravity and settling are not implemented in this lane.

This is a slow, bounded material experiment, not the production game. The normal world remains collision-only. A nonzero crack count alone is not proof of a visible hole.
