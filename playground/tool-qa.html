What these demonstrations establish

A force-limited hand drives the native pick. Ground contact must actually occur near the declared target. The soil cases require at least 30 mm of penetration within the point's length plus one cell; the rock cases require zero excavation. Tool mass and the hand's force limit are checked.

Unsupported means the engine cannot answer that physical question. It does not mean the product passed. The recorded whole/dented state describes only this trial; pending detailed damage is unresolved.

Dry-soil resistance is experimental ground-work-v1. Rock excavation, wear, calibrated material behavior, wet soil and full energy closure remain open. The view shows a section of the actual flat ground; 40 mm tool cells and 100 mm terrain columns limit detail.
