What this range checks

Each specimen starts fresh. Cases check finite results, material-cell identity through fracture, actual contact, valid damage counts and changes against a measured baseline. Baseline changes require review; they do not automatically replace the reference.

This tests the detailed fracture solver. It does not qualify the live-world damage trigger, a complete building, plastic flow, fatigue, grain, or thermal damage. The catalog is experimental. A passed run is not a real-material calibration or a complete energy-conservation test.

Intact world objects already move as rigid bodies. Fine cells are still allocated at world creation; allocating damage detail only when needed remains planned.
